# Equipment checkout

The fictional Atlas Learning Lab lends demonstration adapters for up to three calendar days. Return borrowed adapters to Room Cedar before 16:00 on the due date. A learner may borrow at most two adapters at once. Workstation computers stay inside the workstation room and cannot be checked out. Ask the support desk about a missing adapter, using reference code ORBIT-42. These lending conditions are invented for retrieval tests and do not establish a real library or laboratory policy.
