# Demonstration project deliverables

An Atlas demonstration project includes a public source repository, an automated deployment workflow, a publicly accessible application, and an English technical article. The walkthrough video must last no more than five minutes. The video should demonstrate construction, a working example, and evidence of automated deployment. The submission package lists the article and video URLs. These are fictional handbook requirements for testing the RAG application; the authoritative requirements for any real assignment come from its actual instructor.
