# Workstation reservations

At the fictional Atlas Learning Lab, reserve a workstation through the Atlas booking portal before arriving. Each reservation lasts two hours. A learner may hold one active reservation at a time. Cancel a reservation at least thirty minutes before its starting time if the workstation is no longer needed. The workstation room opens from 08:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Its schedule differs from the library's opening hours. All names and policies in this demonstration handbook are fictional.
