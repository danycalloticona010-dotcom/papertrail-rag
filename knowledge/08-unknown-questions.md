# Questions outside the handbook

The Atlas handbook does not contain information about tuition fees, medical advice, weather forecasts, university admissions, or current political events. For an unsupported question, the application should say that it cannot answer from its documents. A language model may know facts from training, but those facts are not evidence from this handbook. Retrieved excerpts are provided for checking generated answers. The fictional handbook is a small educational corpus, not an authoritative source for real-world decisions.
